# Knowledge Base

The knowledge base is what a Rypl agent knows about your business: prices, products, shipping, returns, opening hours. Agents answer from it instead of guessing, so replies are only as good as what you put in. It's on **every plan**, on the agent's **Knowledge** tab, so each brand or client has its own.

---

## Sources

### Website
Enter your site's address. Replies link there, and the agent can read the page to learn from it. Start with the page that answers the most questions, often the FAQ or a product page. When the page changes, click **Read it again**.

### Q&A
A question customers ask, and the answer you want. Use it for anything the agent must get right every time:

- "Do you ship to Canada?" → "Yes, in 5–8 business days. Shipping is free over $60."
- "Is it true to size?" → "Yes. If you're between sizes, go one up."
- "Can I pay in instalments?" → "Yes, with Klarna at checkout."

The agent still words each reply itself. To send exactly the same text every time, use a [reply rule](reply-rules.md) with **My text** (Pro and Ultra).

### Documents
Three files at most, each up to 3 MB, in PDF, TXT or DOCX, or a CSV product list built from the template in the app. Good for price lists, size charts, a returns policy or a service menu. Use text-based files: Rypl can't read a scanned image of a page and marks such a file as unreadable.

---

## It learns when you save

There is no train button. Save a change and the knowledge base learns it, so the next reply already has the new price or delivery time. The bottom of the **Knowledge** tab shows when it last learned.

---

## What to put in first

1. **The questions you get most**, as Q&A. The 20 to 50 most common ones cover the biggest share of comments.
2. **Prices**: every variant, and any offer running now.
3. **Shipping**: where you deliver, how long it takes, what it costs.
4. **Returns**: how many days, on what conditions, and how to begin one.
5. **Your website** for everything else.

---

## Keep it current

- Update it the same day prices, stock or offers change.
- Before a launch or a sale, add the offer, its dates and its conditions.
- Remove what's no longer true. Two documents with different prices confuse the agent.

---

## Suggest improvements

**Suggest improvements**, under the instructions on the agent's **Persona** tab, goes through the agent's recent replies and the questions it couldn't answer, then drafts new Q&A and changes to the instructions, sometimes a whole knowledge document too. You add each with a click. It doesn't make up facts about your business: where an answer needs something only you know, it leaves a gap in [brackets] for you to fill in.

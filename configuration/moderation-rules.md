# Moderation

Rypl moderates comments on Facebook and Instagram ads and posts and on TikTok ads. Spam and hate are hidden automatically on every plan; on top of that you choose banned words, a tone filter and, on Pro and Ultra, rules written in plain words. Spam is flagged in about 1.7 seconds on average and hidden within seconds (RYPL's own measurement).

**Hide or delete?** A hidden comment stays visible to the person who wrote it and their friends, so they aren't told and don't escalate. A deleted comment is gone for everyone and can't be restored. TikTok doesn't allow deleting other people's comments, so on TikTok, Delete becomes Hide.

---

## What's on every plan

### Spam and hate
Hidden automatically, with no set-up.

### Strict Spam & Media Filter
A channel switch that also hides comments with links, attachments or competitor mentions, including photo-only comments. Useful on ads that attract link spam.

### Banned words
Words and phrases you never want shown: competitor names, a rival's domain, slurs. Comments containing them are hidden or deleted. Add them in **Global rules** (for every channel) or in a channel's **Moderation** tab (for that channel only). Up to 50 per channel.

How a banned word matches:

| You type | It matches |
|---|---|
| `scam` | Words that start with "scam": *scam*, *scammer*, *scams* |
| `*scam*` | "scam" anywhere, even inside other words |
| `"scam"` | Only the whole word *scam* |

### Filter by tone
For comments that rules and banned words don't catch. Set per channel:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Off** (default) | Leaves comments alone |
| **Hide negative** | Hides rude or angry comments |
| **Delete negative** | Deletes rude or angry comments. Can't be undone. Not on TikTok |
| **Hide all** | Hides every new comment, whatever it says |
| **Delete all** | Deletes every new comment. Can't be undone. Not on TikTok |

Off is the default for a reason: a legitimate complaint answered in public builds more trust than a thread with no criticism at all. Complaints go to **Needs attention** so a person answers them.

### Block author
In a conversation, **Block author** quietly hides that person's new comments on the channel. They aren't blocked on Facebook, so they aren't notified and can still message your Page. **Undo** is one click. For a full ban, use Meta Business Suite.

## Moderation rules in plain words (Pro and Ultra)

A rule is a short card you write the way you would explain it to a new hire, plus what Rypl should do with a match. Rypl reads each comment for meaning, so one rule covers every way of saying the same thing.

**Examples:**

- *Comments with links to other shops* → **Hide**
- *Anything about a refund or a missing order* → **Flag for me**
- *Comments calling us a scam without mentioning an order* → **Hide**
- *Questions about wholesale prices* → **I'll answer myself**

### Actions

| Action | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Allow** | Stays visible. Filter by tone won't touch it |
| **Hide** | Hidden from everyone except the author and their friends |
| **Delete** | Removed for everyone. Can't be undone. Hides instead on TikTok |
| **Flag for me** | Goes to Needs attention for you to decide |
| **I'll answer myself** | The AI stays silent; the comment goes to Needs attention for your reply |

If several rules match, the strictest action wins: Delete, then Hide, Flag, Answer myself, Allow.

### Global rules and channel rules

- **Global rules** work on every Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account you connect. Open them from the **Global rules** button on the Channels page.
- **Only on this channel**: rules you add in a channel's Moderation tab, on top of the global ones.
- To skip a global rule on one channel, uncheck it there.

### Review first or Apply automatically

- **Review first**: matches wait in your inbox until you decide. Start new rules here to see what they catch.
- **Apply automatically**: each rule does what it says; unsure matches are set aside for you.
- **Pause** stops every rule on every channel without deleting them.

## Checking what was hidden

In the [inbox](inbox.md), filter by **All hidden or deleted**, or narrow it down: **Hidden by your rules**, **Hidden by banned words**, **Hidden by tone filter**, **Hidden by you**, **Deleted**, **From blocked authors**. Unhide anything hidden by mistake.

# Multiple Accounts and Pages

One Rypl account can run several brands or clients. Each Facebook Page (with its Instagram account) or TikTok ad account is a **channel** with its own settings; agents and rules can be shared across channels or kept separate.

---

## Plan limits

| | Simple | Pro | Ultra |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Accounts** | 1 | Up to 5 | Unlimited |
| **Channels with AI replies** | Up to 3 | Up to 20 | Unlimited |
| **AI agents** | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |

- An **account** is a Facebook or TikTok login you connect. One login can bring many Pages.
- A **channel** is a Facebook Page with its linked Instagram account, or a TikTok ad account.
- **The channel limit counts AI replies, not connections.** Pages you only moderate cost nothing against it, so a client who wants spam hidden but no replies takes up no channel. Hitting the limit doesn't turn off replies anywhere they're already running.
- **Settings** shows how much of the plan you use, for example *Channels with AI replies 3/20*.

No plan limits the number of comments.

---

## Adding accounts and Pages

1. Open **Channels** and click **Connect channel**.
2. Under Facebook & Instagram, choose **Business profile** (Pages in a Meta Business portfolio, no re-login) or **Personal profile**; or choose **TikTok ads**.
3. Sign in with that account and pick the Pages or ad accounts to add.

To add another Page from a login you've already connected, open the **⋯** menu next to the login, choose **Add or remove Pages** and keep the other Pages ticked. Pages added after sign-up start with **Replies** off.

Agencies usually connect through **Business profile**, so Pages in their clients' Business portfolios stay connected without logging in again.

---

## One agent per brand

Create an agent for each brand or client, with its own instructions, tone and knowledge base, and pick it on each of that brand's rows on **Channels**. One agent can serve several channels of the same brand: its Facebook Page with the linked Instagram account, and its TikTok ad account.

---

## Moderation across channels

- **Global rules**: moderation rules and banned words for every channel at once, in the card at the top of **Channels** (rules on Pro and Ultra; banned words on every plan). **Used on** switches them off for a channel they don't fit.
- **Per channel** (click its row): **Choose which** to skip single global rules or the banned words, **Only on this channel** for extra rules and words, **Filter by tone** and the **Spam & media filter**.
- **Replies on several channels at once**: tick their rows on **Channels** and click **Enable AI** or **Disable AI**.

---

## Working across brands

- **Channels** groups your channels by the login that connected them, so one client's Pages stay together. Filter by **Platform**, **Agent**, **Replies**, **Moderation** or **Status**, or search by name.
- The [inbox](inbox.md) shows every channel; filter by **Channel** or **Platform**.
- Each reply shows which agent wrote it, so you can tell brands apart at a glance.
- **Needs attention** collects what needs a person across every brand.
- On Pro and Ultra, you can connect an AI app to **only selected** channels, for example one client's Pages, through the [API and MCP server](api-and-mcp.md).

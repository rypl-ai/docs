# Brand Voice and Instructions

Two agent settings shape how Rypl sounds: its **tone** and its **instructions**, both on the agent's **Persona** tab. They belong to the agent, so each brand or client you run can sound different. Facts, such as prices and shipping times, go in the [knowledge base](knowledge-base.md) instead.

---

## Tone

Pick one of four tones; the app shows a sample reply for each. The same question, "Do you ship to Canada?", might come back like this:

| Tone | How it sounds | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Neutral** | Plain and professional | "Yes, we ship to Canada in 5 to 8 business days." |
| **Seller** | Steers toward the purchase | "We do, and orders over $60 ship free. Want the link?" |
| **Positive** | Friendly and upbeat | "We sure do! It takes 5 to 8 business days 🙂" |
| **Direct** | As short as possible | "Yes. 5–8 business days." |

---

## Instructions

**Agent Instructions** are plain-language guidance, up to 4,000 characters. Write them like a brief for a new team member who doesn't know your business yet.

**Instructions that work:**

- "Keep replies to two sentences, then add the product link."
- "Say 'pieces', never 'items'."
- "When someone asks about price, mention free shipping over $50."
- "Don't promise delivery dates; quote the range on the shipping page."
- "If you aren't sure, say a teammate will follow up, and stop there."
- "Use the formal 'you' (Sie, vous) in German and French."

**Too vague to help:** "Be nice", "Sound human", "Be helpful".

**Business name**, on the same tab, is what the agent calls your business. Leave it empty and it uses the Page name.

### Add examples

A few example answers teach the agent your style faster than adjectives. Put them in the instructions, or add them as Q&A in the [knowledge base](knowledge-base.md).

| Comment | The reply you want |
|---|---|
| "Does this come in blue?" | "Yes, in navy and sky blue, both in stock right now." |
| "Looks cheap" | "Fair question. It's full-grain leather with a 2-year warranty; happy to share more." |
| "When's the restock?" | "In about two weeks. Message us and we'll let you know when it's back." |

Q&A gives the agent the facts, but it still words each reply itself. To send the exact same words every time, use a [reply rule](reply-rules.md) with **My text** (Pro and Ultra).

## Emojis

**Emojis in replies** is a channel setting: click the channel's row on **Channels** to find it. When it's on, the agent adds an emoji now and then, where it fits. Replies sent by a **My text** rule go out exactly as you wrote them.

---

## Links

How often a reply carries a link to your site is set under **Links** on the **Replies** tab, with four choices: **When it helps the answer**, **In every reply**, **Rarely** and **Never**. **Shorten links** keeps the URLs short. Links need your website on the **Knowledge** tab.

---

## Where the agent replies

**Replies to**, on the agent's **Replies** tab:

| Option | The agent answers |
|---|---|
| **Comments and messages** (default) | Comments, Messenger and Instagram DMs |
| **Comments only** | Comments; you handle DMs |
| **Messages only** | DMs; you handle comments, which are still moderated |

---

## How soon

| Option | When replies go out |
|---|---|
| **Immediately** (default) | Seconds after the comment. In RYPL data for Q3 2026, 97% of Facebook replies were out within a minute |
| **Within a few minutes** | About a minute or two later |
| **Within an hour** | Somewhere between 20 and 50 minutes later |
| **Within a day** | Somewhere between 2 and 6 hours later |

Facebook may throttle a new Page that replies the moment a comment arrives, so on a new Page *Within a few minutes* is the safer start.

---

## Check the result

1. Save your changes.
2. Read the next replies in the Inbox, under **AI replies**.
3. Adjust the instructions, not individual replies, until it sounds right.

**Suggest improvements**, under the instructions, also drafts edits to them from the questions your customers actually asked.

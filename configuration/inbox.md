# Inbox and Needs Attention

Rypl's **Inbox** gathers the comments and messages from all your connected Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and TikTok ad accounts in one list, with the replies that were sent and the comments that were hidden. It covers your whole history, not only recent items.

---

## Views

The sidebar lists five views under **Inbox**:

| View | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Needs attention** | Everything waiting for a person: rule matches, plus negative or urgent comments nobody has answered |
| **Needs review** | Only the comments your rules flagged for you to check |
| **Negative** | Negative comments and messages that are still visible |
| **AI replies** | Everything your agents answered |
| **Hidden or deleted** | Everything taken down, including comments from blocked authors |

---

## What each conversation shows

- The comment or message, with its **intent** label (Question, Purchase, Complaint…) and sentiment.
- **Which agent replied.** Replies you wrote yourself are marked as yours.
- **The comment someone was replying to**, when they answered another commenter.
- Why a comment was hidden, or what your rules want, for example *Needs review: would hide* or *Reply needed*.
- On Facebook, a **Liked** badge on comments your Page liked through Rypl.
- **Translate** and **Block author** in the conversation header, and **View post** to open the ad or post the comment is under.

---

## Needs attention

**Needs attention** collects:

- negative or urgent comments that have no reply, and on channels with **Replies** on, negative or urgent DMs;
- comments matched by a moderation rule set to **Flag for me** (*Needs review*) or **I'll answer myself** (*Reply needed*);
- with rules in **Review first**, every rule match, labelled with what the rule would do, for example *Needs review: would hide*.

Its **Rules** filter narrows the list to **Needs review** or **Reply needed**.

How items leave the list:

- **Negative or urgent comments**: once you or the agent reply, once they are hidden or deleted, or once you open the conversation.
- **Needs review** and **Reply needed**: when you hide or delete the comment or click **Mark as reviewed**; a *Reply needed* item also goes once you've answered it.

The dashboard shows how many are open on its **Needs attention** tile.

## What you can do

| Action | Notes |
|---|---|
| Reply | To comments and DMs |
| Edit a reply | After it's posted, on Facebook and TikTok. Instagram doesn't allow it |
| Hide / Unhide | Takes the comment out of public view on Facebook, Instagram or TikTok, or brings it back |
| Delete | Permanent. On TikTok it works only on your own replies |
| Mark as reviewed | Comments flagged *Needs review* or *Reply needed* |
| Block author / Unblock | In the conversation header: hides that person's comments on the channel. Not a Facebook or TikTok ban |
| Translate | In the conversation header: shows the comment in another language |

**Many at once.** Tick conversations, or the box at the top and then **Select everything that matches**, and use **Read**, **Reviewed**, **Hide** or **Delete** in the bar above the list. Unhiding works one comment at a time.

**DMs.** Meta gives the agent 24 hours from a person's last message to answer. After that you can usually still answer by hand for up to 7 days, if Meta allows it for your connection. Once that has passed, the Inbox marks the messaging window as expired.

**Instagram DMs another app handles.** If a chatbot or helpdesk owns an Instagram conversation, a reply you send from the Inbox takes it over, provided the Page lets Rypl take control in Meta's Instagram conversation routing. If it doesn't, Rypl holds the reply back and tells you what to switch on.

## Filters

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| Channel | One Page or ad account |
| Platform | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok |
| Type | Comments, Messages |
| Sentiment | Positive, Neutral, Negative |
| Intent | Question, Purchase, Request, Complaint, Feedback, Sharing, Spam |
| Language | One language at a time |
| Moderation | Hidden by your rules, Hidden by banned words, Hidden by channel filters, Hidden by you, Deleted, From blocked authors, Not hidden or deleted |
| Time | Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days |

To find a word in any comment or message, use **Search messages** above the list (the **/** key takes you there).

---

## Dashboard

The **Dashboard** sums up the same activity for today, 7 days, 30 days or dates you pick: open **Needs attention** items, **AI replies** sent, **Reply coverage** (the share of comments and messages that got a reply), **Hidden or deleted**, **Sentiment**, **Questions** and **High priority**. Below the tiles, **Activity** charts incoming against answered and shows when people write most.

## Ask an AI app about your inbox

On Pro and Ultra you can connect an AI app such as Claude Code or Cursor and ask about the Inbox in plain words: what customers complained about this week, which questions are still unanswered, what's waiting in Needs attention. See [API and MCP server](api-and-mcp.md).

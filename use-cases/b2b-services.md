# B2B and Services

Service businesses (clinics, studios, agencies, SaaS, local services) use ads to get leads. The comments under those ads are people asking what it costs, whether you cover their area and how to book. An answer while they are still on the ad keeps the lead; an answer the next day usually doesn't.

---

## The knowledge base

- **Your website.** Service pages, pricing or price ranges, areas served, the booking page.
- **Files.** A service menu, terms, a one-page overview: up to 3 documents (PDF, TXT or DOCX).
- **Q&A.** "How much is a consultation?", "Do you work with companies outside the EU?", "How do I book?", with the answer you want given.

If prices depend on the job, say so in the knowledge base and give the next step ("Prices start at $X; book a free call here: [link]"). The agent can then answer honestly instead of guessing a number.

---

## Agent instructions for a professional tone

Examples of instructions that work:

- "Answer in two sentences at most, then give the booking link."
- "Never quote a final price; give the starting price and offer a call."
- "Don't give medical, legal or financial advice. Say a specialist will reply and stop."
- "Use formal address in German and French."

Of the four tones, **Neutral** is the clear, professional one.

---

## Sending leads and issues to a person

Leads and issues that need a person go to the **Needs attention** view in the inbox:

- **Complaints** that only describe a problem go there by default, as do negative or urgent comments nobody has answered.
- On Pro and Ultra, a **moderation rule** set to **Flag for me** or **I'll answer myself** catches what you define in plain words, for example: *Comments asking for a quote for more than 10 people* → **I'll answer myself**.
- **Reply rules** (Pro and Ultra) can keep the agent silent on comments with certain words, or answer with your own text, such as your booking link for comments that mention booking.

A person always handles legal threats and emergencies.

---

## Messages

Agents answer Messenger and Instagram Direct messages as well as comments, following the agent's instructions (reply rules and moderation apply to comments only).

- **The 24-hour window.** Meta gives the agent 24 hours from the person's last message to answer. Past that, your team can still reply from the inbox for up to 7 days, provided your connection has Meta's Human Agent permission, so pick up open DMs quickly.
- **A helpdesk or chatbot on Instagram.** If another app is the default app for your Instagram DMs, Rypl leaves those conversations to it. Choose which app should answer: see [Instagram DMs go to another app](../troubleshooting/facebook-connection.md#instagram-dms-go-to-another-app).

---

## What to measure

- How fast questions under your ads get a reply.
- Leads that reached Needs attention, and how fast someone followed up.
- Cost per lead in Ads Manager, before and after.

On Pro and Ultra, a connected AI app can answer some of this for you, such as "which questions has nobody answered?" See [API and MCP server](../configuration/api-and-mcp.md).

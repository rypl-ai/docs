# Use Cases

[Rypl](https://www.rypl.is) works the same way for every business: agents answer comments and DMs from your knowledge base, filters and rules handle the noise, and anything that needs a person waits in Needs attention. What changes is what you put in the knowledge base and which rules you write.

---

## Guides

### [E-Commerce](ecommerce.md)

Stores and direct-to-consumer brands selling through Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ads. Price, size, stock and shipping questions; scam accusations and competitor links; complaints about orders.

### [Marketing Agencies](agencies.md)

Agencies running ads for several clients. One agent and knowledge base per client, global rules plus rules per channel, and plans sized by accounts and pages.

### [B2B and Services](b2b-services.md)

Service businesses and B2B companies using ads for leads: questions about pricing, availability and how to book, answered while the person is still on the ad, with qualified leads and complaints sent to a person.

---

## What changes by business type

| | E-commerce | Agency | B2B and services |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Most common comments** | Price, stock, sizes, shipping | Depends on the client | Pricing, availability, how to book |
| **Knowledge base focus** | Product pages, shipping and returns, current offers | One per client | Services, prices or price ranges, booking steps |
| **Rules worth writing** | Hide scam claims and competitor links; send order complaints to review | Global rules for everyone, rules per client channel | Send "talk to sales" requests to review |
| **Typical plan** | Simple for one store, Pro for several pages and rules | Pro for up to 5 accounts, Ultra for unlimited | Simple or Pro |

---

## The same for everyone

1. **Fill the knowledge base before you go live.** Your website, the questions you get most, and your policies.
2. **Be specific in the agent's instructions.** Length, tone, what to say when it doesn't know.
3. **Check the first week closely.** Read replies and hidden comments every day, and fix gaps in the knowledge base.
4. **Keep complaints with a person.** Use Needs attention for anything that needs judgment.

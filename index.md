# Rypl Documentation

**[Rypl](https://www.rypl.is)** is AI software that replies to and moderates comments and DMs on Facebook and Instagram, plus comments on TikTok ads. AI agents answer buyers in your brand voice from your own knowledge base, spam and hate are hidden by the moderation Rypl sets up from your business description, and complaints wait for a person in one inbox. Plans start at $19 a month, with no limit on comments and a [7-day free trial](https://app.rypl.is/signup).

These pages walk through setting Rypl up and getting good results from it. For a short answer to one specific question, with screenshots of the app, see the Rypl help center at **[rypl.is/docs](https://www.rypl.is/docs)**.

---

## Quick start

1. **[Create your account](getting-started/sign-up.md)** and start the 7-day free trial.
2. **[Connect your channels](getting-started/connect-facebook.md)**: Facebook Pages with their linked Instagram accounts, and TikTok ad accounts.
3. **[Set up an AI agent](getting-started/create-ai-agent.md)**: brand voice, knowledge base and how it replies.
4. **[Go live](getting-started/go-live.md)**: pick an agent for each channel, switch on **Replies** and check the first results.

## What Rypl handles

| Platform | Comments | Messages |
|---|---|---|
| **Facebook** | Ads (including dark posts) and Page posts | Messenger |
| **Instagram** | Ads and posts, Reels included | Direct messages |
| **TikTok** | Ads | — |

Every platform is on every plan. On TikTok, Rypl handles comments on ads: organic TikTok posts and TikTok messages are not supported.

## Key facts

| | |
|---|---|
| **Replies** | Under 60 seconds on the default *Immediately* setting; 97% of Facebook replies go out within a minute (RYPL data, Q3 2026) |
| **Spam** | Flagged in about 1.7 seconds on average and hidden within seconds (RYPL's own measurement) |
| **Languages** | Replies in the language each comment is written in, or in one language you set per channel |
| **Plans** | Simple $19, Pro $49, Ultra $89 a month; billed yearly $182, $470 or $854 a year |
| **Plan limits** | Accounts and channels with AI replies: 1 and 3 on Simple, 5 and 20 on Pro, unlimited on Ultra |
| **Comment limit** | None, on any plan |
| **API and MCP server** | Pro and Ultra, free trial included |
| **Connection** | Official Meta Graph API and TikTok Marketing API |

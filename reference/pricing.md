# Pricing

Rypl has three plans with a flat monthly price. **No plan limits the number of comments**, so a month when an ad takes off costs the same as a quiet one. Every plan starts with a **7-day free trial** and covers Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ads.

## Which plan

- **Simple ($19):** one business with up to three pages that wants every question answered and spam hidden.
- **Pro ($49):** several pages or brands, rules written in plain words, replies that read the post. The plan most customers choose.
- **Ultra ($89):** agencies and high-volume brands: unlimited accounts and pages, the most powerful AI, conversation memory and priority support.

---

## Free trial

- 7 days free on every plan: $0 today, no charge until the trial ends.
- Your set-up goes live as soon as the trial starts.
- Cancel any time before the trial ends and you aren't charged.
- One free trial per customer.

---

## Billing

- Prices are in US dollars. Yearly billing is charged once a year and saves about 20%.
- Cancel any time; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
- If you move to a lower plan, your rules stay saved and work again when you upgrade.
- Customers in the EU can withdraw from a contract through the [withdrawal form](https://www.rypl.is/withdrawal).

See [Billing troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/billing.md) for payment questions, and the full pricing page at [rypl.is/pricing](https://www.rypl.is/pricing).

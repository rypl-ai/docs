# Pricing

Rypl has three plans with a flat monthly price. **No plan limits the number of comments**, so a month when an ad takes off costs no more than a quiet one. Every plan starts with a **7-day free trial** and covers Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ads.

---

## Plans

| | **Simple** | **Pro** (most chosen) | **Ultra** |
|---|:--:|:--:|:--:|
| **Monthly** | $19/mo | $49/mo | $89/mo |
| **Billed yearly** | $182 a year ($15/mo) | $470 a year ($39/mo) | $854 a year ($71/mo) |
| **Accounts** | 1 | Up to 5 | Unlimited |
| **Channels with AI replies** | Up to 3 | Up to 20 | Unlimited |
| **AI agents** | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Comments** | No limit | No limit | No limit |

**What the limits count:**

- **An account** is a Facebook or TikTok login you connect to Rypl. One Facebook login can bring several Pages.
- **A channel** is a Facebook Page together with its linked Instagram account, or a TikTok ad account. The Instagram account doesn't count on its own.
- **Only channels with AI replies switched on count.** Connecting more channels than that is allowed, and moderation runs on every one of them; a channel used for moderation only doesn't use up the plan.

## Which plan

- **Simple ($19):** one brand or creator with AI replies on up to 3 channels, who wants questions answered and spam hidden.
- **Pro ($49):** several brands or clients, moderation rules in plain words, reply rules, replies that read the post, and the API and MCP server. The most chosen plan.
- **Ultra ($89):** agencies and large brands: no limit on accounts or channels, the most powerful AI, conversation memory and priority support.

---

## Free trial

- 7 days free on every plan: $0 today, no charge until the trial ends. Checkout asks for a card.
- Everything you set up starts working the day the trial begins.
- Cancel any time before the trial ends and you aren't charged.
- One free trial per Rypl account. If you've had one, you're charged when you subscribe.

---

## Billing

- Prices are in US dollars, before tax: checkout adds sales tax or VAT for your country.
- Yearly billing is charged once a year and costs about 20% less than paying monthly.
- **Upgrading** happens in **Settings**. On a paid plan you pay the prorated difference for the rest of the period, and the higher plan starts right away. During the trial, nothing is charged until the trial ends.
- **Moving to a smaller plan** isn't a button in Settings: email [hey@rypl.is](mailto:hey@rypl.is) from the address you sign in with and name the plan you want. Moderation rules stay saved on Simple but only run again on Pro or Ultra.
- **Cancelling:** Rypl keeps working until your paid period runs out, and your agents, rules and channels are held for 60 days after that.
- Customers in the EU can withdraw from a contract through the [withdrawal form](https://www.rypl.is/withdrawal).

See [Billing troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/billing.md) for payment questions.

# Changelog

Notable changes to [Rypl](https://www.rypl.is). Every item is live in the app at [app.rypl.is](https://app.rypl.is). How each feature works today is in the [Rypl help center](https://www.rypl.is/docs); the story behind bigger releases is on the [Rypl blog](https://www.rypl.is/blog).

---

## November 2026

| Date | What changed | Plans |
|------|--------------|-------|
| 12 Nov | **API and MCP server.** Connect Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI or your own script in **Settings → MCP and API Keys**, then ask about your comments and DMs, count them or export them. You choose what each app may read, on which channels and for how long. With your permission an app can also hide and unhide comments or edit your moderation rules. No app can reply or delete | Pro, Ultra (free trial included) |

Details: [API and MCP server](configuration/api-and-mcp.md) and [How to Get Facebook, Instagram and TikTok Ad Comments Through an API or MCP (2026)](https://www.rypl.is/blog/facebook-instagram-tiktok-comments-api).

## October 2026

| Date | What changed | Plans |
|------|--------------|-------|
| 26 Oct | **TikTok accounts.** Besides TikTok ads, you can connect a **TikTok account** in **Connect channel**. Rypl then answers, hides and can like comments on that account's own posts, Spark Ads that run as it included. TikTok messages aren't supported | Every plan |
| 19 Oct | **A new Channels page.** One **Connect channel** menu for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok ads; each channel's row has its agent and a **Replies** switch that apply at once; clicking a row opens its reply and moderation settings; **Global rules** is a card at the top of the page | Every plan |
| 19 Oct | **Inbox views in the sidebar**: Needs attention, Needs review, Negative, AI replies, and Hidden or deleted. Select many conversations and mark them read or reviewed, hide or delete them in one go | Every plan |
| 19 Oct | **Instagram DMs another app handles.** When a chatbot or helpdesk owns a Page's Instagram DMs, Channels says so, and the Page's **Instagram DMs** setting leaves them to that app or lets Rypl take them over (beta). A reply you send from the Inbox takes the conversation over when the Page allows it | Every plan |
| 19 Oct | The agent setting *Where it replies* is now called **Replies to** | Every plan |
| 12 Oct | **Plan limits count channels with AI replies**: 3 on Simple, 20 on Pro, unlimited on Ultra, next to 1, 5 or unlimited accounts. Connect every Page you manage; moderation runs on all of them | Every plan |
| 12 Oct | **The Rypl help center** opens at [rypl.is/docs](https://www.rypl.is/docs): setup, agents, moderation, reply rules, the inbox, billing and troubleshooting, one question per page | Everyone |
| 3 Oct | Plan cards describe what each plan does, without AI model names. Models upgrade automatically on every plan | Every plan |
| 2 Oct | Reply context: the inbox shows the comment someone was replying to; one click opens their conversation | Every plan |
| 1 Oct | Agents with faces: see which agent replied and what each did while you were away | Every plan |
| 1 Oct | Liked badge on Facebook comments your Page liked through Rypl | Every plan |
| 1 Oct | Light theme (Settings → Appearance) | Every plan |
| 1 Oct | What's new in the sidebar; an agent cube guides you through setup | Every plan |

Details: [What's New in RYPL: Moderation Rules, Reply Rules and AI Agents (October 2026)](https://www.rypl.is/blog/whats-new-in-rypl-october-2026).

## March 2026

- **TikTok ads.** Comments on TikTok ads are answered and moderated on the same engine as Facebook and Instagram. TikTok doesn't allow deleting other people's comments, so Rypl hides them. At launch this covered ads; TikTok accounts followed in October 2026. Details: [Rypl Now Supports TikTok Ads: AI Comment Management for Advertisers](https://www.rypl.is/blog/rypl-now-supports-tiktok-ads).
- **Instagram Direct replies.** Agents answer Instagram DMs as well as comments.

## February 2026

- **Auto-replies 2.0.** Replies answer from your knowledge base, read the intent behind each comment and reply in the commenter's language. Details: [Auto-Replies 2.0: Smarter, Faster, Context-Aware](https://www.rypl.is/blog/auto-replies-v2).
- **Instagram.** Comments on Instagram ads and posts in the same dashboard as Facebook.

## 2025

- **First release.** AI replies and moderation for Facebook comments, through the official Meta Graph API.

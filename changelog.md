# Changelog

Notable changes to [Rypl](https://www.rypl.is). Every item is live in the app at [app.rypl.is](https://app.rypl.is). For the full story of each release, see the [Rypl blog](https://www.rypl.is/blog).

---

## October 2026

| Date | What changed | Plans |
|------|--------------|-------|
| 3 Oct | Plan cards describe what each plan does, without AI model names. Models upgrade automatically on every plan | Every plan |
| 2 Oct | Reply context: the inbox shows the comment someone was replying to; one click opens their conversation | Every plan |
| 1 Oct | Agents with faces: see which agent replied and what each did while you were away | Every plan |
| 1 Oct | Liked badge on Facebook comments your Page liked through Rypl | Every plan |
| 1 Oct | Light theme (Settings → Appearance) | Every plan |
| 1 Oct | What's new in the sidebar; an agent cube guides you through setup | Every plan |

Details: [What's new in RYPL, October 2026](https://www.rypl.is/blog/whats-new-in-rypl-october-2026).

## March 2026

- **TikTok ads.** Comments on TikTok ads are answered and moderated on the same engine as Facebook and Instagram. TikTok doesn't allow deleting other people's comments, so Rypl hides them. Details: [Rypl now supports TikTok ads](https://www.rypl.is/blog/rypl-now-supports-tiktok-ads).
- **Instagram Direct replies.** Agents answer Instagram DMs as well as comments.

## February 2026

- **Auto-replies 2.0.** Replies answer from your knowledge base, read the intent behind each comment and reply in the commenter's language. Details: [Auto-Replies 2.0](https://www.rypl.is/blog/auto-replies-v2).
- **Instagram.** Comments on Instagram ads and posts in the same dashboard as Facebook.

## 2025

- **First release.** AI replies and moderation for Facebook comments, through the official Meta Graph API.

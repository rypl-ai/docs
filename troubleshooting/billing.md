# Billing

---

## The free trial

- Every plan starts with **7 days free**: $0 today, and no charge until the trial ends.
- Settings shows how many days of the trial remain.
- Cancel before the trial ends and you aren't charged.
- One free trial per customer. If you had a trial before, checkout shows **Billed from today**.

---

## Prices and billing periods

| Plan | Monthly | Billed yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Simple | $19 | $182 a year ($15/mo) |
| Pro | $49 | $470 a year ($39/mo) |
| Ultra | $89 | $854 a year ($71/mo) |

Prices are in US dollars; taxes may apply depending on where you are. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, so charges and receipts come from Lemon Squeezy on behalf of Rypl.

---

## Changing plans

Choose a different plan in **Settings**. Moving up unlocks the higher plan's features. If you move down:

- your rules stay saved, and work again when you upgrade;
- keep within the lower plan's limits on accounts, pages and agents (Simple: 1 account, 3 pages, 1 agent; Pro: 5 accounts, 20 pages).

---

## Cancelling

- Cancel any time from **Settings → Manage subscription**. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
- Your agents, knowledge base and rules are kept, so you can pick up where you left off if you come back.
- Customers in the EU can withdraw from a contract through the [withdrawal form](https://www.rypl.is/withdrawal).

---

## A payment failed

Rypl shows **Payment required**. Click **Update payment method** in **Settings** and enter a working card.

---

## Refunds and invoices

- Invoices and receipts are in the billing portal: **Settings → Manage subscription**.
- For a refund request, a double charge or a charge you don't recognise, email [hey@rypl.is](mailto:hey@rypl.is) with your account email, the date and the amount.

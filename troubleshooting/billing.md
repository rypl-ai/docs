# Billing

---

## The free trial

- Every plan starts with **7 days free**: $0 today, and no charge until the trial ends. Checkout asks for a card.
- Settings shows how many days of the trial remain.
- Cancel before the trial ends and you aren't charged.
- One free trial per Rypl account. If you had a trial before, checkout shows **Billed from today** and you're charged when you subscribe.
- When the trial ends, the plan continues and the first payment is taken; your set-up carries on as it is.

---

## Prices and billing periods

| Plan | Monthly | Billed yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Simple | $19 | $182 a year ($15/mo) |
| Pro | $49 | $470 a year ($39/mo) |
| Ultra | $89 | $854 a year ($71/mo) |

Prices are in US dollars, before tax: checkout adds sales tax or VAT for your country. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, so charges and receipts come from Lemon Squeezy on behalf of Rypl.

## Upgrading

Open **Settings**: each higher plan has an **Upgrade** button, labelled **Switch** while you're on the trial.

- **During the trial**, nothing is charged until the trial ends.
- **On a paid plan**, you're charged the difference between the two plans for the rest of the current period, and the higher plan applies immediately. Settings shows the amount before you confirm, and after that each period is billed at the new price.
- **On a yearly plan**, expect the real charge to come out above the estimate Settings shows; the receipt has the exact figure.

A partial charge on your statement usually means an upgrade in the middle of a billing period.

---

## Moving to a smaller plan

There's no downgrade button in Settings. Email [hey@rypl.is](mailto:hey@rypl.is) from the address you sign in with and say which plan you want. Before you move down, keep within the smaller plan's limits: Simple covers 1 account, AI replies on 3 channels and 1 agent; Pro covers 5 accounts and 20 channels. Moderation rules stay saved on Simple and run again when you move back to Pro or Ultra.

---

## Cancelling

- Cancel from **Settings**: the **Manage** button on the **Subscription** card opens the billing portal. Rypl keeps working until your paid period runs out.
- Rypl holds on to your agents, rules and channels for 60 days once the plan has ended. Pick a plan in that window and everything switches back on; later, they may be removed.
- Deleting the Rypl account leaves the subscription running, so cancel before you delete.
- Customers in the EU can withdraw from a contract through the [withdrawal form](https://www.rypl.is/withdrawal).

---

## A payment failed

A failed payment pauses Rypl right away: no replies and no moderation until it's fixed. The app tells you the last payment didn't go through, and Settings shows **Payment required**.

1. Click **Update payment method** on the **Dashboard**, or **Manage** on the **Subscription** card in Settings.
2. Enter a working card in Lemon Squeezy's billing portal, which opens in a new tab.
3. Once the payment goes through, usually in under a minute, Rypl starts again.

Comments posted while the payment was failing aren't answered or moderated later.

---

## Refunds and receipts

- Each charge comes with a receipt that Lemon Squeezy emails to you. Card changes go through **Manage** on the **Subscription** card in Settings.
- For a refund request, a double charge or a charge you don't recognise, email [hey@rypl.is](mailto:hey@rypl.is) with your account email, the date and the amount.

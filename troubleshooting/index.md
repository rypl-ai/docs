## Quick checks

1. **Is Replies on?** On **Channels**, the channel's row needs an agent and the **Replies** switch on.
2. **Is the account connected?** **Login expired** or a **Reconnect** button on **Channels** means sign in again. The **Status** filter lists every channel that needs reconnecting.
3. **Is the plan active?** **Payment required** in Settings means the last payment failed, and Rypl has stopped replying and moderating.
4. **Is Facebook limiting the Page?** A line under the Page's name on **Channels** says so, with the time left.
5. **Was the comment hidden?** Inbox → **Hidden or deleted**.
6. **Is it waiting for you?** Check **Needs attention**.

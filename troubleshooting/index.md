# Troubleshooting

---

## Guides

### [Connection problems](facebook-connection.md)
A Page or Instagram account doesn't appear, permissions were declined, an account shows **Expired** or **Needs reconnect**, TikTok won't connect.

### [Agent not replying](agent-not-replying.md)
Comments go unanswered, replies are late or vague.

### [Billing](billing.md)
The free trial, changing plans, cancelling, failed payments and refunds.

---

## Quick checks

1. **Is Reply with AI on?** Channels → the channel → AI replies.
2. **Is the account connected?** An **Expired** or **Needs reconnect** badge means sign in again.
3. **Is the plan active?** **Payment required** in Settings means the last payment failed.
4. **Was the comment hidden?** Inbox → filter **All hidden or deleted**.
5. **Is it waiting for you?** Check **Needs attention**.

# Comment Moderation Best Practices

Good moderation removes what hurts the ad (spam, scams, abuse) and keeps what helps it: questions, praise and complaints that get a public answer. Every viewer reads the thread before deciding whether to click.

---

## Hide, answer or escalate

| Comment | What to do | How in Rypl |
|---|---|---|
| Spam, bot text, link spam | Hide | Automatic; add **Strict Spam & Media Filter** for ads that draw link spam |
| Scam links, fake "support" accounts | Hide | Automatic, plus banned words or a rule |
| Hate, slurs, threats | Hide (or delete) | Automatic, plus banned words |
| Competitor names and links | Hide | Banned words, or a rule: *comments recommending another store* → Hide |
| Questions about price, stock, shipping | Answer | The agent answers by default |
| Complaints about an order | A person answers | **Needs attention** by default; a rule can **Flag for me** |
| Refund and return requests | A person answers, or the agent with your policy | Agent answers from the knowledge base; a rule can send them to you |
| Praise | Thank | **Thank-you replies** (Pro and Ultra) or **Like positive comments** (Facebook) |
| A lone emoji, friends tagging friends | Leave | Nothing to set |

---

## Hide, don't delete, by default

A hidden comment stays visible to its author and their friends, so they don't know and don't come back angrier. Hiding can be undone; deleting can't. Keep **Delete** for comments you never want anyone to see, such as slurs or doxxing. On TikTok, delete isn't possible for other people's comments, so Rypl hides them.

---

## Leave legitimate complaints visible

A complaint answered in public shows every later viewer that you respond. A thread with no criticism at all looks curated. That's why **Filter by tone** is off by default and complaints go to **Needs attention**. Turn on **Hide negative** only for ads that attract hostility unrelated to your product, such as political arguments.

---

## Banned words that don't catch customers

- Use quotes for short words: `"ass"` matches only the whole word.
- Without quotes, a word matches words that start with it: `scam` also catches *scammer*.
- Use asterisks only when you mean "anywhere": `*casino*`.
- Add competitor names in every spelling buyers use.
- Check **Hidden by banned words** in the inbox weekly for innocent comments.

---

## Rules you can trust (Pro and Ultra)

- **Write rules like instructions to a new hire.** *Hide comments that recommend another store or include a discount code that isn't ours.*
- **One idea per rule.** Two short rules are easier to check than one long one.
- **Start in Review first.** Matches wait for you labelled with what the rule would do. Switch to **Apply automatically** when the rule is right almost every time.
- **Write global rules once** for what applies to every brand, and add rules **Only on this channel** for one brand.
- **Use I'll answer myself** for comments the AI should never touch, such as legal threats or press enquiries.

---

## Review what was hidden

Every week, open the inbox filter **All hidden or deleted** and skim it. Unhide mistakes, then fix the cause: a banned word that's too broad, a rule that's too vague, or a tone filter that's too strict.

# Comment Moderation Best Practices

Good moderation removes what hurts the ad (spam, scams, abuse) and keeps what helps it: questions, praise and complaints that get a public answer. Every viewer reads the thread before deciding whether to click.

---

## Hide, answer or escalate

| Comment | What to do | How in Rypl |
|---|---|---|
| Spam, bot text, link spam | Hide | The moderation Rypl sets up at sign-up; the **Spam & media filter** also catches links, attachments and photo-only comments |
| Scam and phishing links, fake "support" accounts | Hide, or delete if you're sure | Banned words for known domains, or a rule (Pro and Ultra) |
| Hate and slurs | Hide (or delete) | Banned words, or a rule (Pro and Ultra) |
| Competitor names and links | Hide | Banned words, the **Spam & media filter**, or a rule (Pro and Ultra): *comments recommending another store* → Hide |
| Questions about price, stock, shipping | Answer | The agent answers by default |
| Complaints about an order | A person answers | **Needs attention** by default; a rule can **Flag for me** |
| Refund and return requests | A person answers, or the agent with your policy | The agent answers from the knowledge base; a rule can send them to you |
| Praise | Thank | **Thank-you replies** (Pro and Ultra) or **Like positive comments** (Facebook) |
| A lone emoji, friends tagging friends | Leave | Nothing to set |

The sign-up builds starting moderation from your business description. Channels you connect after sign-up begin with **Filter by tone** and the **Spam & media filter** switched off, so open each new channel's **Moderation** settings on **Channels**.

---

## Hide, don't delete, by default

A hidden comment stays visible to its author (on Facebook, to their friends as well), so they rarely notice and don't come back angrier. Hiding can be undone; deleting can't. Keep **Delete** for what must go for good: scams and phishing links, someone's personal data posted in public, or content you must remove for legal reasons. On TikTok, delete isn't possible for other people's comments, so Rypl hides them.

---

## Leave legitimate complaints visible

A complaint answered in public shows every later viewer that you respond. A thread with no criticism at all looks curated. That's why **Filter by tone** is off by default and complaints go to **Needs attention**. Turn on **Hide negative** only for ads that attract hostility unrelated to your product, such as political arguments, and protect comments you always want kept with a rule set to **Allow**: it shields them from **Hide negative** and **Delete negative**.

---

## Banned words that don't catch customers

- Banned words act at once, without AI, and rules can't override them. Up to 50 in **Global rules** and 50 more per channel; each list has its own action: **Hide**, **Delete** or **Off**.
- Use quotes for short words: `"ass"` matches only the whole word.
- Without quotes, a word matches words that start with it: `scam` also catches *scammer*.
- Use asterisks only when you mean "anywhere": `*casino*`.
- Add competitor names in every spelling buyers use.
- Keep banned words for exact spellings. For something people can say many ways, a moderation rule catches the meaning.

---

## Rules you can trust (Pro and Ultra)

- **Write rules like instructions to a new hire.** *Hide comments that recommend another store or include a discount code that isn't ours.*
- **One idea per rule.** Two short rules are easier to check than one long one. When several match, the strictest action wins.
- **Add rules in Review first.** **Global rules** has a single **When a rule matches** setting that covers every rule. Matches wait in **Needs review**, labelled with what the rule would do. Switch to **Apply automatically** when your rules are right almost every time.
- **Write global rules once** for what applies to every brand. On a channel, **Choose which** unticks single global rules, and **Only on this channel** adds rules for that brand alone.
- **Use I'll answer myself** for comments the AI should leave to you, such as press enquiries or wholesale prices.
- **Know what rules skip.** They read comments, not DMs, and skip photo- or video-only comments, which the **Spam & media filter** handles. A new rule applies to comments posted after you save it.

---

## Review what was hidden

Every week, open **Hidden or deleted** in the inbox sidebar and skim it. The **Moderation** filter narrows it to what your rules, banned words, channel filters or you took down. Unhide mistakes one at a time, then fix the cause: a banned word that's too broad, a rule that's too vague, or a tone filter that's too strict.

## Quick tips

1. **Fill the knowledge base before you go live.** The questions you get most, as Q&A, cover the biggest share of comments.
2. **Keep complaints with a person.** Leave **Filter by tone** off unless your ads draw real hostility; let complaints reach **Needs attention**.
3. **Start new rules in Review first.** Switch to Apply automatically once a rule catches the right comments.
4. **Use quoted banned words for short words.** `"ass"` matches only the whole word; `ass` would also catch *assist*.
5. **Fix the source, not the reply.** A wrong answer means a missing or outdated fact in the knowledge base.
6. **Read the first week closely.** Every reply and every hidden comment, every day.

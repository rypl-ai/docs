# Better AI Replies

A good reply to an ad comment answers the actual question, in a sentence or two, in your voice, with a next step. Three things decide whether Rypl's replies get there: the knowledge base, the instructions and the rules.

---

## 1. The knowledge base decides accuracy

The agent can only state facts it has. Most weak replies trace back to a missing or outdated fact.

- **Start with Q&A** for the 20 to 50 questions you get most, with the answer you want given. The agent still words each reply itself; when the wording must be exact, use a **My text** reply rule (Pro and Ultra).
- **Add prices, variants and offers.** "How much?" is the most common question under ads.
- **Add shipping and returns**, by country if they differ.
- **Add your website** for everything else; links in replies point there. Click **Read it again** after the site changes.
- **Add documents** for what isn't on the site: up to 3 files of 3 MB each (PDF, TXT, DOCX or a CSV product list). Use text-based files; a scanned page can't be read.
- **Update the same day** something changes. The knowledge base learns when you save; there is no training step.

---

## 2. Instructions decide the style

**Agent Instructions** take up to 4,000 characters. Write them like a brief to a new team member:

- **Length:** "Two sentences at most for simple questions."
- **Next step:** "End with the product link when someone asks about a product."
- **Words:** "Say 'pieces', never 'items'."
- **Limits:** "Never promise delivery dates." "Never discuss pricing for wholesale; say a teammate will reply."
- **When unsure:** "If the answer isn't in the knowledge base, say a teammate will reply."

Pick the **tone** that fits: Neutral, Seller, Positive or Direct. Most consumer brands do well with Neutral or Positive; Seller works for promotions; Direct suits brands with a clipped voice.

Two settings shape replies outside the instructions. **Links**, on the agent, sets how often a reply links to your website: when it helps the answer, in every reply, rarely or never. **Emojis in replies**, on the channel, lets the agent add an emoji now and then.

---

## 3. Rules decide what gets a reply (Pro and Ultra)

- Add **My text** replies for questions asked word for word again and again. Give a rule several versions (up to 10) and Rypl picks one per comment, so the thread doesn't fill with identical replies.
- For thank-you replies to kind comments, switch on **Praise and support** in the agent's **What it replies to** section.
- Use **Don't reply** for topics a person should handle in private.
- Turn on **Read the post** under **Smarter replies**, so "how much is this?" gets the price of the product in the ad.
- On Ultra, **Remember the conversation** lets the agent pick up where an earlier conversation with the same person left off.

Before you save a reply rule, its preview shows how it would have treated recent comments on your channels.

---

## 4. Improve it every week

1. Read a sample of replies under **AI replies** in the inbox: are the facts right, is the tone right, is there a next step?
2. Run **Suggest improvements** (on the agent's **Persona** tab, below the instructions). It goes through what the agent recently said and what it had no answer for, then drafts Q&A and instruction edits, with [brackets] where only you know the fact.
3. When the same comment keeps coming in, Rypl suggests answering it with your own text; accept it where the answer never changes.
4. Fix the source (knowledge base or instructions), never just the one reply.

---

## Signs something needs fixing

| What you see | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| "Check our website" instead of an answer | Fact missing | Add it as Q&A |
| Wrong price | Outdated document | Update or remove it |
| Too long | No length instruction | Add one |
| Wrong product | The post wasn't read | Turn on **Read the post** (Pro and Ultra) |
| Wrong formality in German or French | No instruction | Say *du/Sie*, *tu/vous* |
| Same reply on every emoji | Broad rule | Add several **My text** versions or narrow the rule |
| **Facebook is limiting replies** on the Page | Replies look automated or too frequent to Facebook | Set **How soon** slower, reply to fewer comments, vary wording with several **My text** versions |

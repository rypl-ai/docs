# Connect Your Channels

A **channel** in Rypl is a Facebook Page (together with the Instagram account linked to it) or a TikTok ad account. The Facebook or TikTok login you connect them with is an **account**. Rypl connects through the official Meta Graph API and TikTok Marketing API: you sign in with Meta or TikTok yourself and choose what Rypl may handle. Rypl never sees your password, and there is no scraping or browser automation.

---

## Facebook

1. In Rypl, open **Channels** and click **Connect channel**. If nothing is connected yet, click **Connect Facebook** on the **Facebook & Instagram** card instead.
2. Choose how to connect:
   - **Business profile** (recommended): through your Meta Business portfolio. The connection is more stable and doesn't ask you to log in again. The usual choice for agencies and for brands that keep their Pages in a portfolio.
   - **Personal profile**: the Facebook profile that manages the Page. You may have to log in again now and then, for example after changing your password.
3. Sign in to Facebook and allow every permission Rypl asks for. They let Rypl read the comments and messages on your Pages, reply to them, and hide or delete comments. None of them reach your ad budgets or payment methods.
4. Select the Pages Rypl should handle.

Connect with the profile or Business portfolio that manages the Page. If a Page doesn't appear, see [Facebook connection troubleshooting](../troubleshooting/facebook-connection.md).

## Instagram

Instagram connects through the Facebook Page it is linked to: connect the Page, and its Instagram professional account comes with it. Rypl then handles comments on its ads and posts (Reels included) and its Direct messages, which include replies to your Stories. On **Channels**, the Instagram account shows under the Page's name and uses the same agent.

If it doesn't show up, check that it is a professional (business or creator) account and that it is linked to the Page in Meta Business Suite, then choose **Reconnect** in the login's **⋯** menu on **Channels**.

**When another app owns your Instagram DMs.** Meta lets one app at a time handle an Instagram conversation. If a chatbot or helpdesk already does, the Page's row on **Channels** says *Instagram DMs go to another app*. Click it to keep that app for DMs or to let Rypl take them over (beta). Comments and Messenger aren't affected.

---

## TikTok

1. On **Channels**, click **Connect channel** and choose **TikTok ads**. If nothing is connected yet, click **Connect TikTok** on the **TikTok ads** card.
2. Sign in to TikTok for Business, authorize Rypl and pick the ad accounts it should handle.
3. Choose an agent and switch on **Replies** on each ad account's row, as you would for a Page.

For comments on your own TikTok posts, connect the account itself too: choose **TikTok account** in **Connect channel**, sign in on TikTok's page and authorize Rypl. It's a separate channel with its own agent and **Replies** switch, and it also takes the comments under Spark Ads that run as that account. TikTok messages are not supported. TikTok doesn't allow deleting other people's comments, so on TikTok Rypl hides them instead. When you first connect, comments from about the last 24 hours can come in as well.

## Plan limits

| | Simple | Pro | Ultra |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounts (Facebook or TikTok logins) | 1 | Up to 5 | Unlimited |
| Channels with AI replies | Up to 3 | Up to 20 | Unlimited |

You can connect more channels than that: the limit counts only channels with AI replies switched on, and moderation works on every channel you connect. An Instagram account linked to a Page is part of that Page's channel, not an extra one. When you try to switch on replies past the limit, Rypl asks you to switch them off on another channel or upgrade.

---

## Managing channels

**Channels** lists your channels grouped by the login that connected them. From there you can:

- pick a channel's agent and switch its **Replies** on or off, right on its row;
- click a row to open that channel's reply and moderation settings;
- add Pages from a login you already connected: **⋯** next to the login, then **Add or remove Pages** (for TikTok, **Add or remove ad accounts**). Keep your other Pages ticked;
- **Reconnect** a login when Facebook or TikTok asks you to sign in again, from the same **⋯** menu;
- **Remove this login**, which stops all its channels and erases their history in Rypl. It can't be undone.

There is no button to remove a single Page. To stop Rypl on one, switch off its **Replies** and its moderation, or untick it under **Add or remove Pages**: its row then reads *No longer shared with RYPL*, and its history and settings are kept in case you tick it again.

Pages you pick during sign-up start with replies on. Pages you add later start with **Replies** off.

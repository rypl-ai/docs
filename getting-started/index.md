# Getting Started with Rypl

Setting up [Rypl](https://www.rypl.is) takes a few minutes. You describe your business, Rypl drafts an agent and moderation for it, you connect your channels, and you choose where the agent answers.

---

## Four steps

### 1. [Create your account](sign-up.md)
Sign up at [app.rypl.is](https://app.rypl.is/signup) with Google or email, describe your business and check the setup Rypl drafts. Every plan starts with 7 days free.

### 2. [Connect your channels](connect-facebook.md)
From **Connect channel**: Facebook Pages through Meta (the Instagram accounts linked to them come along), and TikTok ad accounts through TikTok for Business.

### 3. [Set up an AI agent](create-ai-agent.md)
Check the agent Rypl built: its instructions, tone and knowledge base, and where and how soon it replies.

### 4. [Go live](go-live.md)
On **Channels**, pick the agent on each channel's row and switch on **Replies**. Then read the first replies and check what was hidden.

---

## What you need

- The Facebook profile, or the Meta Business portfolio, that manages the Page you want to connect. Instagram accounts must be professional (business or creator) accounts linked to that Page.
- For TikTok, access to the ad account in TikTok for Business.
- Your website, and the answers to the questions customers ask most.

There's nothing to install: Rypl runs in the browser, and nothing goes on your website or into your ads.

---

## What happens after launch

- **Questions** get an answer from your knowledge base, in the commenter's language, in under 60 seconds on the default *Immediately* setting.
- **Spam and hate** are hidden by the banned words, filters and rules Rypl set up from your description. Spam is flagged in about 1.7 seconds on average and hidden within seconds (RYPL's own measurement).
- **Every comment gets an intent label**: a question, a purchase, a complaint and so on.
- **Complaints and rule matches** wait for a person in **Needs attention**.
- **Only new comments** are handled. Anything posted before you connected and switched Rypl on stays as it was.

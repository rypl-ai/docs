# Getting Started with Rypl

Setting up [Rypl](https://www.rypl.is) takes a few minutes. You describe your business, Rypl builds a starting agent and rules, you connect your channels and switch it on.

---

## Four steps

### 1. [Create your account](sign-up.md)
Sign up at [app.rypl.is](https://app.rypl.is/signup), describe your business in a few sentences and pick a plan. Every plan starts with 7 days free.

### 2. [Connect your channels](connect-facebook.md)
Connect Facebook Pages (and the Instagram accounts linked to them) through Meta, and TikTok ad accounts through TikTok.

### 3. [Set up an AI agent](create-ai-agent.md)
Check the agent Rypl built: its instructions, tone and knowledge base (website, Q&A, documents). Review the moderation set-up.

### 4. [Go live](go-live.md)
Switch on **Reply with AI** for your channels, then read the first replies and hidden comments.

---

## What you need

- A Facebook account with admin access to the Page you want to connect. Instagram accounts must be professional accounts linked to that Page.
- For TikTok, access to the TikTok ad account (TikTok for Business).
- Your website and the answers to the questions customers ask most.

---

## What happens after launch

- **Questions** get an answer from your knowledge base, in the commenter's language, in under 60 seconds on the default setting.
- **Spam and hate** are hidden automatically. Spam is flagged in about 1.7 seconds on average and hidden within seconds (RYPL's own measurement).
- **Every comment is labelled** with what it wants: a question, a purchase, a complaint and so on.
- **Complaints and rule matches** wait for you in **Needs attention**.

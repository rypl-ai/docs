# Sign Up for Rypl

---

## Create your account

1. Go to **[app.rypl.is/signup](https://app.rypl.is/signup)** and sign up with Google, or with an email address you confirm first.
2. **Answer a few questions**: where your comments come from, and who you're setting Rypl up for.
3. **Describe your business** in your own words, in any language: what you sell, what customers ask, how replies should sound, what should be hidden and what you'd rather answer yourself. Add your website if you have one, then click **Build my setup**.
4. **Check the setup.** Rypl drafts an agent (instructions, tone and a few Q&A answers) together with moderation rules and banned words. Edit any of it, or say what to change and Rypl rebuilds it. Nothing goes live until you turn it on.
5. **Connect one account**: Facebook (Instagram comes with its linked Page) or TikTok. You can add more once the trial has started, up to your plan's limit.
6. **Choose a plan** and start the trial. Rypl then switches on for the Pages you picked, and new comments start getting answers.

Moderation rules run on Pro and Ultra. Banned words and filters run on every plan, so on Simple the drafted rules stay saved for when you upgrade.

## Choosing a plan

| | Simple | Pro | Ultra |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $19/mo | $49/mo | $89/mo |
| **Billed yearly** | $182 a year ($15/mo) | $470 a year ($39/mo) | $854 a year ($71/mo) |
| **Accounts** | 1 | Up to 5 | Unlimited |
| **Channels with AI replies** | Up to 3 | Up to 20 | Unlimited |
| **AI agents** | 1 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Replies to comments and DMs, knowledge base, intent detection** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Spam and hate hidden automatically, banned words, Filter by tone, author blocking** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Reads the post, moderation rules, reply rules, thank-you replies** | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| **API and MCP server** | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Remembers the conversation, most powerful AI** | — | — | ✅ |
| **Priority support** | — | — | ✅ |

- An **account** is a Facebook or TikTok login you connect. A **channel** is a Facebook Page with its linked Instagram account, or a TikTok ad account.
- Only channels with AI replies switched on count toward the limit. You can connect more, and moderation works on all of them.
- No plan limits the number of comments.
- Prices are in US dollars, before tax: checkout adds sales tax or VAT for your country.

Which plan fits:

- **Simple**: one brand or creator that wants questions answered and spam hidden on up to three channels.
- **Pro**, the most chosen plan: several brands or Pages, rules written in plain words, replies that read the post, and the [API and MCP server](../configuration/api-and-mcp.md).
- **Ultra**: agencies and large brands, with no account or channel limit, the most powerful AI and conversation memory.

To upgrade later, open **Settings**: you pay the prorated difference for the rest of the period and the new plan starts at once. There's no downgrade button; to move to a smaller plan, write to [hey@rypl.is](mailto:hey@rypl.is) from the address you sign in with. Full details: [Pricing](../reference/pricing.md).

## The 7-day free trial

- Every plan starts with **7 days free**: $0 today, and no charge until the trial ends. Checkout asks for a card.
- Your setup goes live as soon as the trial starts, and Settings shows how many days are left.
- Cancel any time before the trial ends and you aren't charged.
- One free trial per customer. If you've had one before, the first payment is taken when you subscribe.

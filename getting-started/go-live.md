# Go Live

---

## Switch it on

1. Open **Channels**.
2. On a channel's row, pick your agent and switch on **Replies**. There's no Save button: the change applies at once. For several channels at once, tick their rows and click **Enable AI**.
3. Click the row to check the channel's settings. Under **Replies**: **Reply language**, **Emojis in replies** and, on Facebook, **Like positive comments**. Under **Moderation**: **Global rules**, **Filter by tone**, the **Spam & media filter** and rules **Only on this channel**.

Your plan sets how many channels can have replies on: 3 on Simple, 20 on Pro, unlimited on Ultra. At the limit, Rypl asks you to switch replies off on another channel or upgrade.

Moderation works with **Replies** off, so a channel can run on moderation only. Every change applies to new comments; earlier ones aren't answered.

---

## What happens next

- **Questions, purchase questions and requests** get a reply from your knowledge base, in the commenter's language. On the default *Immediately* setting, replies go out in under 60 seconds; 97% of Facebook replies go out within a minute (RYPL data, Q3 2026).
- **Spam and hate** are hidden by your banned words, filters and rules. Spam is flagged in about 1.7 seconds on average and hidden within seconds. Counting the time Meta takes to deliver a comment, 94% of the Facebook comments Rypl hid were down within a minute of being posted (RYPL data, Q3 2026).
- **Every comment gets an intent label**: Question, Purchase, Complaint and so on.
- **Negative or urgent comments without a reply**, and rule matches set to *Flag for me* or *I'll answer myself*, wait in **Needs attention**. Legal threats and emergencies always wait for a person.
- On Facebook, **Like positive comments** lets your Page like positive comments automatically.

---

## Your first day

- Open **AI replies** in the Inbox sidebar and read what went out. Each reply shows which agent wrote it.
- Open **Hidden or deleted** and check what was taken down. Unhide anything hidden by mistake, then fix the banned word or rule that caught it.
- Note answers that were off and add the missing facts to the [knowledge base](../configuration/knowledge-base.md). Fix the source, not one reply.

---

## Your first week

- Run **Suggest improvements** on the agent: it drafts Q&A from the questions customers actually asked.
- If your rules are in **Review first**, look at what they flagged under **Needs review**. Once they catch the right comments, switch the Global rules card to **Apply automatically**.
- Adjust the tone or instructions if replies sound off.
- Note your ROAS and cost per result in Ads Manager, so you can compare later. See [Measuring results](../best-practices/roas-improvement.md).
- On Pro and Ultra, connect an AI app such as Claude Code or Cursor and ask it what customers complained about this week. See [API and MCP server](../configuration/api-and-mcp.md).

---

## When a person should step in

- Complaints about an order, refunds and anything sensitive.
- Legal, medical or safety questions.
- A thread that is turning into an argument.

From the Inbox you can reply, hide, unhide or delete at any time, and edit a posted reply on Facebook and TikTok. **Block author** hides a person's comments on that channel without banning them on Facebook or TikTok.

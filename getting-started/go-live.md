# Go Live

---

## Switch it on

1. Open **Channels** and pick a channel.
2. In **AI replies**, switch on **Reply with AI** and choose your agent.
3. In **Moderation**, check your banned words, **Filter by tone** and, on Pro and Ultra, your rules.
4. Save. Changes apply to new comments after you save.

Moderation works even with **Reply with AI** off, so a channel can run on moderation only.

---

## What happens next

- **New questions** get a reply from your knowledge base, in the commenter's language. On the default *Immediately* setting, replies go out in under 60 seconds; 97% of Facebook replies go out within a minute (RYPL's own measurement).
- **Spam and hate** are hidden automatically. Spam is flagged in about 1.7 seconds on average and hidden within seconds (RYPL's own measurement).
- **Every comment gets a label**: Question, Purchase, Complaint and so on.
- **Complaints and rule matches** set to *Flag for me* or *I'll answer myself* wait in **Needs attention**.
- On Facebook, **Like positive comments** lets your Page like positive comments automatically; liked comments carry a **Liked** badge in the inbox.

---

## Your first day

- Read every reply in the [inbox](../configuration/inbox.md). Each one shows which agent wrote it.
- Open the filter **All hidden or deleted** and check what was hidden. Unhide anything hidden by mistake.
- Note questions the agent answered badly, and add the missing facts to the [knowledge base](../configuration/knowledge-base.md). Fix the source, not one reply.

---

## Your first week

- Run **Suggest improvements** on the agent: it turns real customer questions into new Q&A.
- If you started rules in **Review first**, look at what they flagged. When a rule catches the right comments, switch to **Apply automatically**.
- Adjust the tone or instructions if replies sound off.
- Note your ROAS and cost per result in Ads Manager, so you can compare later. See [Measuring results](../best-practices/roas-improvement.md).

---

## When a person should step in

- Complaints about an order, refunds and anything sensitive.
- Legal, medical or safety questions.
- A thread that is turning into an argument.

You can reply, hide, unhide or delete from the inbox at any time (and edit replies on Facebook), and **Block author** hides a person's new comments on that channel without blocking them on Facebook.

# Rypl Documentation

*Last updated: October 11, 2026*

Official documentation for **[Rypl](https://www.rypl.is)**, the AI tool that replies to and moderates comments and DMs on Facebook and Instagram, plus comments on TikTok ads. AI agents answer buyers in your brand voice from your own knowledge base, spam and hate are hidden automatically, and complaints wait for a person in one inbox. Plans start at $19 a month with no limit on comments and a [7-day free trial](https://app.rypl.is/signup).

**Read the docs: [rypl-ai.github.io/docs](https://rypl-ai.github.io/docs/)**

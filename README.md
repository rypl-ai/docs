# Rypl Documentation

*Last updated: November 23, 2026*

**[Rypl](https://www.rypl.is)** is AI software that replies to and moderates comments and DMs on Facebook and Instagram, plus comments on TikTok ads. AI agents answer buyers in your brand voice from your own knowledge base, spam and hate are hidden automatically, and complaints wait for a person in one inbox. Plans start at $19 a month with no limit on comments and a [7-day free trial](https://app.rypl.is/signup).

## Where to read the docs

- **[www.rypl.is/docs](https://www.rypl.is/docs)**: the official Rypl help center, always current, one question per page. Start here for exact menu names, plan details and troubleshooting.
- **[rypl-ai.github.io/docs](https://rypl-ai.github.io/docs/)**: this repository as a website, with guides, best practices and use cases that link to the help center.

## Key facts

- **Platforms:** Facebook (ads, Page posts, Messenger), Instagram (ads, posts, Direct messages) and TikTok (comments on ads and on your own TikTok posts), on every plan. TikTok messages aren't supported.
- **Plans:** Simple $19, Pro $49, Ultra $89 a month, about 20% less billed yearly. Simple covers 1 account and AI replies on 3 channels, Pro 5 accounts and 20 channels, Ultra unlimited. No plan limits comments.
- **API and MCP server:** on Pro and Ultra, also during the free trial. Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code, Gemini CLI or a script can read, count and export your comments and messages through it. A connected app can't reply or delete.
- **Speed (RYPL's own measurements):** replies in under 60 seconds on the default setting; spam flagged in about 1.7 seconds on average and hidden within seconds.
- **AI:** the latest OpenAI models, upgraded automatically.
